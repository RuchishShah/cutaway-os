Starship · Structure

Nosecone

A one-piece ogive cap welded from stainless steel gores. It carries the forward flap hinge loads, so the inside is heavily reinforced with longerons and ring frames around the attachment points. The very tip is not empty volume — the LOX header tank forms it.
