Starship · Thermal

Heat shield

About eighteen thousand hexagonal ceramic tiles cover the windward face. Hexagons are used so there is no continuous straight gap for plasma to run along. Tiles pin onto studs welded to the hull; since Flight 4 an ablative backup layer sits underneath in case one is lost.
