Starship · Propulsion

Hot-gas thrusters

Attitude control in vacuum. Early prototypes used cold gas — nitrogen simply blown out of a nozzle — but that is weak and needs its own tank. These burn gaseous methane and oxygen tapped from the main tanks instead, which is roughly five times more efficient and reuses propellant the vehicle already carries.
