Booster barrel

The tank wall is the airframe. Rings of stainless steel roughly 1.8 m tall are rolled, welded into barrels and stacked — the visible horizontal seams are those welds. Steel was chosen over carbon fibre because it keeps its strength when cryogenic and when hot.
