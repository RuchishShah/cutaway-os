Autogenous pressurisation

As propellant drains, something has to fill the space or the tank collapses. Most rockets carry high-pressure helium to do it. Starship instead taps hot gas from the engines — gasified methane into the methane tank, gasified oxygen into the LOX tank. No helium, no COPVs full of it, and nothing that can run out independently of the propellant.
