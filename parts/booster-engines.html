33 × Raptor 3

Thirty-three Raptors in three rings: an inner triangle of 3, a middle ring of 10, and an outer ring of 20. The inner 13 gimbal; the outer 20 are fixed. Only the centre 3 stay lit through hot staging, and only those 3 relight for the boostback and landing burns.
